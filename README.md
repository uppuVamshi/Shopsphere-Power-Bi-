# Shopsphere-Power-Bi-
## 📊 Project Overview
An end-to-end data analytics project that uses SQL for data extraction, cleaning, transformation, and analysis and Power BI for interactive dashboard development, visualization, and business insights.

The project contains three dashboards:

**1. Executive Dashboard** -- overall business performance and
revenue/profit analysis.

**2. Customer Retention Dashboard** -- customer segments, repeat
customers, customer value, and purchasing behavior.

**3. Product & Return Dashboard**-- product sales, profitability,
returns, return reasons, and revenue lost due to returns.

The dashboards use interactive slicers and visualizations to help users
explore business performance by category, region, payment method,
customer segment, product, city, and date.

 ## 🎯 Business Questions Answered

The project answers important business questions such as:

### Sales & Profitability
- What is the total revenue generated?
- What is the total profit?
- Which categories generate the highest revenue?
- Which products are most profitable?
- Which brands generate the highest revenue?
- Which regions perform best?
  ### Customer Analytics
- How many customers does the business have?
- How many customers are repeat customers?
- What is the customer retention rate?
- Who are the top customers by revenue?
- Who are the top customers by profit?
- Which customer segments generate the most revenue?
- How frequently do customers place orders?
  ### Product & Return Analytics
- Which products sell the most?
- Which products generate the highest profit?
- Which categories have the most returned orders?
- Which products are returned most frequently?
- What are the major return reasons?
- How much revenue is lost due to returns?

## 📈 Key Business Insights
- Electronics is the strongest revenue-generating category.
- Apple is one of the leading brands by revenue.
- Galaxy A55, MacBook Air, Pavilion 14, and iPhone 15 are among the highly profitable products shown in the dashboard.
- The business has a high proportion of repeat customers.
- 38.97M in revenue is shown as lost due to returns.
- Major return reasons include damaged products, products not meeting expectations, quality issues, defective products, and wrong items.
- Revenue and profitability vary significantly across regions, cities, categories, brands, and products.
